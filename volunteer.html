Join the Quiet Haven Team

We are always looking for empathetic students to help us maintain our safe space.

What do volunteers do?

You don't need to be a mental health professional to help! Your main role is simply to be a friendly presence and ensure the space remains a calm and welcoming environment.

  • Welcoming participants: Greeting them gently, showing them around the safe space, and explaining how things work.
  • Offering light support: Being available to answer questions, listen, or just offer a reassuring presence.
  • Protecting the calm: Helping to ensure our "no forced interaction" rule and low sensory load are respected.
  • Assisting with activities: Helping set up quiet reading areas, board games, or creative workshops.

Note: Voluntary work are for the full day.
