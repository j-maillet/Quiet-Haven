The Avoidant Personality Disorder Problem

What is Avoidant Personality Disorder?

People with AvPD (Avoidant Personality Disorder) do not lack the desire for social connection; In fact, their problem is that they are paralyzed by the fear of being mocked, rejected, or judged. To them, such experiences are painful and even unbearable.

Who is affected by AvPD?

AvPD affects approximately 1.5% to 2.5% of the global population.

In a country like France, this translates to over 1 million people living in deep social isolation — a silent, invisible reality.

It is found in nearly 10% to 15% of psychiatric outpatients, though often misdiagnosed as simple social anxiety or depression.

AvPD affects men and women equally, showing that the need for a safe space is universal and not determined by gender.

Our Solution: The Quiet Haven

We provide a physical and emotional refuge for students suffering from social isolation. A place to breathe, without any social pressure, and reconnect at your own pace.
