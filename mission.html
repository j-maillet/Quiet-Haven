Our Mission & Concept

Who are we helping?

  • Primary Target (18–35 years old): This is the "high-stakes" period. This age group faces the most social pressure (university, first jobs, romantic relationships). A safe place acts as a bridge to prevent total social withdrawal. As a project rooted at IUT Lyon 1, this is our core audience.
  • Secondary Target (35–55 years old): Focuses on reconnection for those who have lived in isolation for years and wish to break the cycle of loneliness. This group often has more resources and a stronger motivation to change their situation. While our space is campus-based, we welcome anyone who feels the need for a calm and judgment-free environment.

The Calm Concept

The group will be defined by its atmosphere Calm rather than just its activities. We provide a physical environment designed to reduce anxiety:

  • Low Sensory Load: Soft lighting, quiet surroundings, and no forced participation.
  • Concrete Spaces: A silent reading corner, creative art tables, and resting spots.
  • Social Pacing: Allowing members to exist in the same space without the immediate pressure to perform or shine socially.

Who are we?

The Quiet Haven is a project created by Swan Gridel, Julien Maillet & Anis Tarras-Rhondali. As students (G3S2B), we realized the heavy impact of social pressure and isolation. We created this safe space to offer the breathing room we believe everyone deserves.

Where to find us?

To ensure maximum accessibility, our refuge is conveniently located directly on campus at IUT Lyon 1, transforming a dedicated space in Building 2 into a peaceful haven for our students.
