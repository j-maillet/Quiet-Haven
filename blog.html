Project Timeline

6 September 2025 – Project Announcement

We are proud to announce the creation of a safe place for people living with Avoidant Personality Disorder. The project aims to provide a calm and supportive environment for individuals struggling with social isolation. Our goal is to create a space where participants feel accepted without pressure or judgment.

The story begins with an unlikely twist: Julien won 250 million euros in the lottery. Rather than keeping it to himself, his first instinct was to do something meaningful — he reached out to Swan and Anis, and together, the three of them decided to build something they truly believed in. That is how The Quiet Haven was born.

15 October 2025 – Identifying Our Target Audience

Our primary target group is young adults aged 18 to 35 years old. This age range faces important social challenges related to studies, employment, and relationships. To help individuals understand their own social anxiety and see if they might be affected by AvPD, we highly recommend reading about it and taking a self-assessment.

Note: This self-assessment is not a medical diagnosis. If you have concerns about your mental health, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Read & Take the Test: AvPD Assessment

5 November 2025 – Establishing Our Visual Identity

To ensure our environment and communications reflect the soothing and welcoming nature of The Quiet Haven, we developed a comprehensive graphic charter. This document outlines our calming color palette, gentle typography, and the overall design principles that guide our project.

View Our Graphic Charter (PDF)

20 November 2025 – Designing The Safe Place

The environment will be designed to reduce stress and anxiety. Soft lighting, quiet spaces, and comfortable furniture will create a peaceful atmosphere. Clear rules and predictable schedules will help participants feel secure. Every visitor will participate at their own pace.

10 January 2026 – Developing Activities

We are preparing activities that encourage social interaction without pressure. Planned activities include reading sessions, board games, creative workshops, and small discussion groups. These activities are designed to help participants gradually build confidence.

See our activities in action on Instagram

5 March 2026 – Recruiting Volunteers

The recruitment of volunteers has begun. We are looking for empathetic individuals who want to support people affected by Avoidant Personality Disorder. Their role will be to create a welcoming and respectful atmosphere.

Become a volunteer

18 April 2026 – Partnership With Professionals

Psychologists and mental health professionals will join the project as advisors and supervisors. Their expertise will help ensure the emotional safety of participants. They will also support volunteers through guidance and training. Professional involvement is essential to the success of the safe place.

15 May 2026 – Community Awareness Campaign

The project will be promoted through schools, universities, and medical centers. Posters and social media campaigns will help spread awareness about AvPD. The objective is to reach both potential participants and future volunteers. Increasing public understanding of AvPD remains a key priority.

View our Awareness Campaign Poster

15 June 2026 – Official Opening

The safe place has officially opened its doors to participants. Visitors are welcomed into a calm, non-judgmental environment designed specifically for people living with Avoidant Personality Disorder. Activities, volunteer support, and professional supervision are all available from day one. This marks the beginning of our mission to reduce isolation and promote belonging.
